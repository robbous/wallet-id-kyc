ePassport Check for Android

A prototype by Roberto Carisi that reads the chip in a passport or a Dutch driving licence and checks it against the printed document. Everything runs on the phone. The app has no internet permission.

Install

Download ePassportCheck-1.0.apk (36 MB, Android 9 or later, phone with NFC)

  • Open this page on the Android phone and tap the download link.
  • When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings, then Install unknown apps).
  • Google Play Protect may say the app is from an unknown developer and offer to scan it. Choose to install anyway: the build is signed by Roberto, not published on Google Play.
  • Make sure NFC is switched on (Settings, then Connected devices or Connections).

Test a Dutch driving licence

  • Tap Take photo and photograph the side of the licence with the one-line code that starts with D1NLD. Lay it flat and avoid glare.
  • Check the app selected Driving licence and shows a green check digit. If the code was misread, correct it in the MRZ line field.
  • Tap Read licence chip, then hold the back of the phone flat on the centre of the card and keep still until the result appears. The NFC antenna position varies by phone: if nothing happens, slide the card slowly around the back of the phone.
  • Scroll down for the comparison and the chip integrity result.
  • Whatever the result, tap Share diagnostics and send the file to Roberto. It contains the read steps and certificate details only: no names, dates or document numbers.
