Privacy policy

ePassport Check

ePassport Check is a personal prototype by Roberto Carisi. It checks whether the printed page of an identity document matches the data on its chip, and whether that chip is authentic. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

Last updated
4 November 2026
Contact
carisi.roberto@gmail.com

In short

The app has no server and makes no network connections. Nothing you photograph or read from a chip leaves your phone. The developer receives no data from the app unless you send a diagnostics file yourself.

What the app processes, and where

DataWhyWhere it goes
Photo of the documentOn-device text recognition (Apple Vision) to read the machine readable zone, printed fields and face.Held in memory while the app is open. Not saved, not uploaded.
Chip data (name, dates, document number, portrait, certificates)Read over NFC with the key printed on the document, to compare with the photo and verify the chip's signatures.Held in memory while the app is open. Not saved, not uploaded.
Diagnostics logRecords the steps of the last chip read and the integrity results, to help fix read failures.Saved in the app's own storage on your phone. It contains no names, dates, document numbers or machine readable zone. It leaves your phone only if you tap Share diagnostics and choose where to send it.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph the document.
  • NFC: to read the document's chip.
  • Photos: only the picture you pick is shared with the app, through the system photo picker.

TestFlight

The app is distributed through Apple TestFlight. Apple may collect crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer can see that feedback and crash reports in App Store Connect.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, which is only the diagnostics log.
