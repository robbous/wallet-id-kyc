This is a prototype, not a bank

The app demonstrates a bank account opening flow with identity verification. It does not open accounts, move money, or offer financial services. It is distributed only to the developer's own devices and to invited TestFlight testers, all adults who have agreed to take part.

What the app collects

Customers verify their identity in one of two ways: with an ID stored in Apple Wallet, or by photographing an ID and taking a selfie through Entrust Identity Verification. Only the data for the method the customer chooses is collected.

Apple Wallet IDs

When a customer chooses to verify with an ID in Apple Wallet, Wallet shows exactly which details the app is asking for and whether the app intends to store them. Nothing is shared until the customer approves the request with Face ID or Touch ID, and they can decline at any point.

Data received from Apple Wallet is used only to verify age and identity for this account application. It is not used for marketing and is never sold. The only data shared outside the developer's server is the ID photo, which goes to Entrust as described below.

The developer's verification server decrypts the response, confirms each detail matches what the issuing authority signed, and checks the issuer and device signatures. It then returns the details, including the ID photo, to the app, which shows them on the application summary. Neither the server nor the app writes them to disk or to a log.

The verification server runs on Cloudflare Workers, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. as a hosting provider to the developer. All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS, and Cloudflare handles the data only to run the server. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Entrust Identity Verification

Entrust Identity Verification is used in two ways, and each asks for consent before capture starts:

  • After an Apple Wallet verification, the customer takes a short selfie video. Entrust compares it with the ID photo from Apple Wallet, to confirm the person holding the phone is the person on the ID. No document is photographed in this flow.
  • Customers without an ID in Apple Wallet photograph a driver's license or passport and take a selfie instead. The selfie is compared with the photo on that ID.

Wallet ID photos, selfies and document images are deleted from Entrust within 30 days of the test session. Each comparison is a facial scan, carried out by Entrust only to confirm the person presenting the ID is its holder. See Entrust's Facial Scan and Voice Recording Policy and its Data Privacy page.

Sharing results

After a verification, a tester can tap Share results to send a summary image and a text log through the iPhone share sheet, to a destination they choose. Nothing is sent unless the tester taps the button and picks where it goes.

Both files are built on the phone and leave out the holder's details: no name, date of birth, address, document number, ID photo or selfie. They contain:

  • whether each requested item was returned by Apple Wallet, never its value;
  • the name of the issuing authority, which identifies the issuer, not the holder;
  • the result of each check, the steps of the flow with their timings, and the outcome;
  • the device model, iOS version and app version;
  • the Entrust workflow run ID.

The workflow run ID points to the verification record Entrust holds, so it is treated as personal data. The developer uses it only to find that record, to investigate a problem or to delete it on request. The latest summary and log stay in the app's storage on the device until the next verification replaces them or the app is deleted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share personal data for advertising or marketing.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • It does not keep a copy of ID data, images or selfies on the device.

Your choices

  • You can decline the Apple Wallet request, or exit the Entrust capture flow, at any time.
  • You can ask for any data held about you to be deleted, or ask what is held, by writing to the contact address above. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Age

The app is for adults only. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and testers are told through TestFlight before the change applies.
